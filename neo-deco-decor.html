Neo Deco Decor: Modern 1920s Glamour, One Piece at a Time

Neo Deco is Art Deco updated for real modern homes: the glamour of the 1920s — brass, marble, velvet, sunbursts and bold geometry — built from individual, shoppable pieces instead of a full renovation. One piece at a time, a plain room turns into a 1920s daydream that still works on a Tuesday.

The five pillars

Warm brass. Brass is the signature metal — sunburst mirrors, globe pendants, cabinet pulls, picture lights. Warm and golden, never chrome-cold. See our brass small upgrades for the weekend-sized version.

Real stone. One marble surface per room is enough: a red marble tray on the bathroom vanity, a white marble side table beside the sofa. See the red marble bathroom and living room roundups.

Jewel-tone velvet. Emerald, navy or deep red velvet on one anchor piece — a loveseat, a chair, a headboard — against cream walls. One jewel tone per room keeps it glamorous instead of busy.

Bold geometry. Sunbursts, scalloped fans, chevrons and trellis on rugs, pillow covers and wall art. Pattern lives on soft surfaces, not on every piece of furniture.

Black and gold. The high-contrast pairing that ties it all to the 1920s — a bar cart dressed in black and gold is the classic example, in our bar cart ideas.

Start in one room

Pick the room you actually live in and give it one anchor, one light and one brass accent — that is the whole method, in detail in How to Get the Neo Deco Look Without Renovating. The living room is the easiest first room: a velvet loveseat, a trellis rug, a marble side table and gold starbursts on the wall.
